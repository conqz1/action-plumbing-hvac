Who We Are

30 Years of Showing Up & Doing It Right

Action Plumbing Heating & HVAC has been serving homeowners in Bethlehem, Allentown, and the greater Lehigh Valley since 1995. What started as a one-truck operation has grown into a trusted local team — but we've never lost the family-first attitude that built our reputation.

As a veteran-owned business, we bring discipline and pride to every job. Our licensed master plumbers don't cut corners, and our 30-day labor warranty backs that up in writing.
